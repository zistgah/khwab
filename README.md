# Khwab @ Zistgah
## Creative Cycler Teaser

A Zistgah audiovisual teaser associated with the **Creative Cycler**.

> **Khwab @ Zistgah — Live your dream.**

The teaser presents a deliberately surreal progression through contrasting cinematic and musical registers. A formal, period-inspired confrontation gives way to a vivid Punjabi musical sequence and then to a dreamlike bedroom scene. The contrasts are part of the creative demonstration.

## What this repository represents

`zistgah/khwab` is an artifact repository for the teaser and its associated metadata and presentation resources.

It should not be confused with the complete Creative Cycler implementation.

The broader Creative Cycler concept is an orchestration layer for creative work. A creative spine can be decomposed into stages; prompts or instructions can be routed to selected creative systems; outputs can be returned to the Cycler; and subsequent stages can incorporate those outputs. The same creative process can extend across text, image, audio and video.

## Creative principles represented by the teaser

- **Cycling rather than single-shot generation** — creative work progresses through successive states.
- **Multimodal continuity** — a common creative spine can move between textual, visual, musical and cinematic forms.
- **Contrast as a creative operation** — cultural, historical, tonal and aesthetic registers can change within one work.
- **Dream as a creative space** — `Khwab` provides the conceptual frame for moving between otherwise incompatible registers.
- **Human direction** — the creative process remains directed by the creator rather than being reduced to autonomous generation.
- **Artifact as evidence** — the published teaser records what the creative process produced; architectural claims require separate evidence.
