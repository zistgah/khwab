# CONTRACT overlay — zistgah/khwab

Thin overlay on the master contract at `zistgah/governance`. It ADDS; it never relaxes.

1. **The audiovisual artifact is canonical.** A defect discovered later becomes a NEW artifact/version rather than silently changing the identity of an already published artifact.
2. **Preserve the distinction between artifact and architecture.** The teaser may demonstrate creative effects, transitions or narrative concepts; it must not be represented as proof of an implementation capability unless independently established.
3. **Preserve creative provenance.** Do not remove or replace the attribution and repository identity associated with the work.
4. **No fabricated identifiers.** Do not invent DOI, ISBN, version, publication date, release number or other persistent identifiers.
5. **No vendor name in any text layer** — titles, slugs, config, metadata, overlays — unless a source file explicitly requires one as factual provenance.
6. **Assets are data.** Only declared media and figures may be treated as repository assets; undeclared files must not be minted by build/seeding processes.
7. **Nothing pushes or mints without his typed gate.** An AI may prepare the script or artifact; he runs the release operation.
8. **The tool's interface is read, never recalled** — and a discovered verb is not a discovered signature.
9. **Do not overclaim the Creative Cycler.** The repository is a teaser/artifact repository, not by itself the complete implementation of the Cycler.
